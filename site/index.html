The pipeline

From voice to verified answer
in seconds

Every voice note travels through a 7-stage pipeline that understands Telugu, reasons over live data, and verifies every answer before the farmer hears it.

  1. Step 01

    Farmer speaks naturally

    The farmer sends a Telugu voice note on Telegram — no typing, no app to install, no special commands. Just speak.

    Telegram Bot API

  2. Step 02

    Voice becomes text

    The OGG audio is converted and sent to Gnani Prisma v3.0, an Indian-language speech-to-text engine that accurately transcribes Telugu with regional dialects.

    Gnani Prisma v3.0 — STT

  3. Step 03

    Question is understood and routed

    The transcript is classified into one of three tiers — general agricultural knowledge, live weather forecast, or personalised farm advice — so the right data sources are queried.

    Intent classifier · Location resolver

  4. Step 04

    AI reasons over live data

    Relevant weather data (WeatherAPI + IMD warnings), crop knowledge, and conversation context are assembled into a prompt. An LLM — currently Groq-hosted openai/gpt-oss-120b — writes a natural Telugu answer.

    Groq · WeatherAPI · IMD

  5. Step 05

    Every answer is checked

    Deterministic safety checks verify the response before it is delivered. Consequential agrochemical or pesticide advice must be backed by an approved source or routes the farmer to an Agricultural Officer, KVK, or Kisan Call Centre 1800-180-1551.

    Safety guardrails · Evidence checks

  6. Step 06

    Answer becomes natural voice

    The verified Telugu text is sent to Gnani Timbre v2.5 — Kaveri voice, producing a warm, natural-sounding Telugu voice reply optimised for rural listening environments.

    Gnani Timbre v2.5 — Kaveri TTS

  7. Step 07

    Farmer hears the answer

    A voice message is delivered directly to the farmer's Telegram chat. No reading required — the entire interaction is voice-to-voice.

    Telegram voice message

Built responsibly

Advice farmers can trust

KrishiVaani is not a general-purpose chatbot. It is designed specifically for Indian farmers, with hard safety rules baked into the pipeline.

  • Grounded, not hallucinated

    Every weather forecast is sourced from live WeatherAPI data and IMD district warnings — not AI memory. Answers that cannot be grounded in verified data are withheld, not guessed.

  • Safe referral for high-stakes advice

    Pesticide doses, emergency crop disease identification, and legal or financial decisions are routed to qualified Agricultural Officers, KVK scientists, or Kisan Call Centre 1800-180-1551 — never answered without evidence.

  • Telugu-first, farmer-centric design

    KrishiVaani speaks like an experienced agricultural helper, not a government form or automated questionnaire. Responses use natural, conversational Telugu — keeping farmer dignity at the centre.
