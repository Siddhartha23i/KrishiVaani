What You Can Ask KrishiVaani

Essential support for every farming decision

KrishiVaani gives farmers accurate, actionable clarity across four core agricultural needs — grounded directly in government feeds and verified university research.

Govt-Verified Weather

Weather & Rainfall Forecasts

Live WeatherAPI predictions combined with official India Meteorological Department (IMD) district rainfall warnings and temperature trends. Plan sowing, irrigation, and harvest with real data.

Central & State Schemes

Government Schemes & Portals

Accurate rules, official portals, and document checklists for PM-KISAN, state assistance (such as Rythu Bharosa in Telangana), and crop insurance — strictly verified, without false promises.

Agronomic Research

Crop Guidance & Soil Care

Sowing windows, field preparation, and nutrient practices for paddy (వరి), cotton (ప్రత్తి), chilli, and pulses — sourced directly from university-approved package of practices (PJTSAU).

Monsoon Contingency

Drought & Low-Rain Assistance

Actionable contingency advisories when monsoon rains are deficient or dry spells prolong: soil moisture conservation, life-saving irrigation techniques, and immediate referral to local Agricultural Officers.

The Pipeline

From voice to a safe answer, in seconds

Every voice note travels through seven guarded stages that transcribe your speech, route your request, and verify facts before a voice reply is generated.

Step 01

Farmer speaks

Sends a voice note on Telegram, in their own language. No typing, no forms.

Step 02

Voice becomes text

Audio is converted and sent to Gnani Prisma speech-to-text.

Step 03

Question is routed to a tier

Question is classified into general knowledge, live weather, or consequential farm advice.

General Knowledge Model Knowledge

Agricultural concepts, crop cycles, and baseline practices answered freely.

Live Weather Live Data

Real-time WeatherAPI forecasts and IMD district alerts for Telangana.

Farm Advice Approved Evidence Only

Verified agronomic package of practices, or safe referral to experts.

Step 04

AI writes the answer

Groq-hosted language model writes a short answer in the farmer's language (with Sarvam AI supported in codebase). General questions use model knowledge, weather questions use live WeatherAPI data (plus IMD warnings for Telangana), and farm advice uses approved sources only.

Step 05

Safety checks

Deterministic checks block unsafe or ungrounded claims: pesticide doses, scheme eligibility and amounts, prices, yield promises, unapproved or expired sources, wrong location. If there is no approved source, the farmer is referred to the Agricultural Officer, KVK or Kisan Call Centre 1800-180-1551.

Step 06

Text becomes voice

Gnani Timbre (Kaveri voice) speaks the answer in Telugu.

Step 07

Farmer hears the answer

A voice message arrives in the same Telegram chat.

Built Responsibly

Advice farmers can trust

KrishiVaani is designed specifically for agriculture, with deterministic rules baked into every turn.

  • Three tiers, three rules

    General knowledge is answered freely, live weather comes from real data, consequential farm advice needs approved evidence.

  • Honest referral

    When there is no approved source, KrishiVaani says so and refers to the AO, KVK or Kisan Call Centre.

  • Your data

    The bot remembers earlier questions to help you; /forget deletes them. No phone numbers or names are stored.
